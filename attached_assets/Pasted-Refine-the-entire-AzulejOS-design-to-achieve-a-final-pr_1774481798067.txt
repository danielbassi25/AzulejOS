Refine the entire AzulejOS design to achieve a final, premium, editorial-level aesthetic.

DO NOT change structure or features.
Focus only on visual refinement, balance, and identity.

---

CORE DIRECTION:
AzulejOS should feel like a Mediterranean digital artifact:
a mix of Portuguese azulejos, Italian editorial elegance, and modern iOS refinement.

Not a tech product → a curated emotional object.

---

1. PATTERN USAGE (CRITICAL FIX)

- Reduce overuse of tile patterns
- Keep azulejo patterns ONLY in:
  • hero sections
  • key highlight tiles
  • special moments (quotes, milestones)

- Remove patterns from:
  • standard cards
  • lists
  • secondary UI elements

- Introduce clean ceramic / ivory backgrounds

Goal:
Create contrast, breathing space, and visual hierarchy.

---

2. TILE DESIGN (MAKE THEM REAL)

- Tiles must feel like real ceramic objects:
  • subtle texture (very light grain)
  • thin borders
  • soft inner shadows
  • gentle imperfections

- Avoid flat digital look
- Avoid repetitive pattern overlays

Goal:
Each tile should feel tactile and collectible.

---

3. COLOR SYSTEM (DEPTH + WARMTH)

- Primary: deep azulejo blue
- Secondary: dusty / faded blue tones
- Background: warm ivory / ceramic white
- Accent: very subtle beige / gold (minimal, elegant)

- Avoid full-blue screens
- Always create contrast between sections

Goal:
Warm Mediterranean palette, not cold UI.

---

4. TYPOGRAPHY (MAJOR UPGRADE)

- Titles:
  • use high-end editorial serif (Playfair Display / Canela style)
  • large, expressive, emotional

- Numbers (e.g. "1379"):
  • iconic, oversized, elegant
  • strong visual presence

- Body text:
  • clean sans-serif (light, airy)
  • refined spacing

- Use:
  • italics for quotes
  • subtle letter spacing for labels

Goal:
Feel like a luxury magazine, not an app.

---

5. VISUAL HIERARCHY

- Each screen must have ONE dominant element
- Reduce noise in secondary components
- Increase spacing between sections

Examples:
• System → focus on days counter
• Saudade → focus on one memory at a time
• Play → focus on the card

Goal:
Calm, premium, intentional layout.

---

6. DEPTH & LIGHT

- Add soft shadows (not heavy)
- Add subtle light gradients across surfaces
- Introduce gentle glow on important elements

- Avoid flat UI

Goal:
Soft, dreamy, dimensional interface.

---

7. MICRO-INTERACTIONS

- Smooth, slow transitions
- Slight hover glow
- Gentle scale/float on tiles
- Elegant button feedback

Goal:
Everything feels soft, alive, and intentional.

---

8. ICONS & DETAILS

- Use thin, elegant line icons
- Consistent stroke and style
- Avoid generic icon sets

- Add subtle decorative details:
  • fine dividers
  • small ornamental touches (very minimal)

---

FINAL GOAL:

AzulejOS should feel:
- aesthetic
- intimate
- timeless
- feminine but elegant
- artistic but not overloaded

It should look like something between:
• a luxury iOS app
• a Mediterranean home interior
• a personal archive of love

Make it feel unique, recognizable, and emotionally designed.
