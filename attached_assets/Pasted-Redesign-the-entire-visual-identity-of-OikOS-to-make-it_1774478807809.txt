Redesign the entire visual identity of OikOS to make it extremely aesthetic, dreamy, elegant, feminine, and distinctive, while still premium and minimal.

Important direction:
The app should no longer rely on pink tones. Shift the full visual identity toward elegant blue tones only.

New visual direction:
- aesthetic, dreamy, girly in a refined high-end way
- editorial, romantic, soft luxury
- should feel like a mix of:
  - an intimate digital diary
  - a premium iOS app
  - a fashion/editorial object
- not childish
- not generic SaaS
- not plain minimal
- not overly dark

Color palette:
- powder blue
- ice blue
- periwinkle
- mist gray
- pale silver
- soft white
- deep midnight navy for contrast
Avoid pink almost entirely.

Design goals:
- make the app feel highly curated and visually memorable
- give it a strong signature identity
- create a dreamy blue atmosphere across all pages
- increase softness, elegance, and femininity
- make it feel “aesthetic aesthetic”

Global visual upgrades:
- introduce layered blue gradients across backgrounds and cards
- add subtle pearl/glass effects
- use soft glow in blue tones
- create more depth and dimension in cards
- use stronger visual hierarchy
- add more negative space and air
- refine corners, borders, and shadows to feel softer and more luxurious
- add subtle texture/grain for warmth
- improve transitions and animations so everything feels fluid and delicate

Typography:
- titles should feel more editorial, elegant, and expressive
- keep body text readable and clean
- create stronger contrast between headings and content
- make section titles feel like a real brand language

Section-specific direction:

1. System
- should feel like a serene control room
- more ethereal blue gradients
- more refined hero area
- elegant stat cards
- make the quote card stunning and memorable

2. Saudade
- this section should feel cinematic and emotional
- stronger visual identity than the rest
- memory cards should feel like keepsakes, not generic cards
- improve overlays, title placement, spacing, and image treatment
- use dreamy blue-tinted overlays
- make the page feel like an archive of beautiful moments

3. Letters
- should feel precious and intimate
- open letters should feel like preserved pieces
- locked letters should feel sacred, sealed, and desirable
- add subtle blur, glow, and “paper artifact” feeling in a modern way

4. Build
- should feel aspirational and clean
- elegant progress design
- more emotional and lifestyle-oriented, less checklist-like

5. Play
- should feel airy, soft, fun, and beautiful
- improve category pills and question cards
- make the question card feel iconic and share-worthy

6. Score
- should feel polished and stylish
- less like a gamification dashboard, more like a premium shared ritual
- improve score visualization and milestone cards

Bottom navigation:
- redesign the bottom nav to feel more signature and luxurious
- stronger active state
- more glass, blur, softness, and polish
- should feel like a beautiful physical object

Important:
Do not change the app structure or functionality.
This is a visual redesign only.
Focus on making the app much more beautiful, blue-toned, aesthetic, feminine, and emotionally distinctive.
